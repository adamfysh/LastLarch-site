Every seed message, in one browsable table

This is the complete draft content of ARIA-Lex: every early action message currently in the library, laid out for a facilitated review session rather than for use as-is.

This is a draft, not an approved library. Every message here is pre-edited and pre-validated seed text. None of it has been through an adaptation workshop or been signed off by an authority yet. It exists so a facilitator can walk a room through the full set, hazard by hazard or sector by sector, and mark up what should be kept, edited, or dropped on the way to a validated, locally owned protocol.
