About the library

The library provides pre-validated early action messages organised by hazard, sector, severity, and authority level. Where library messages exist for the selected hazard and severity, the AI will use them as reference. Output is tagged to show whether messages are from the library, contextualised from it, or synthesised where no library entry exists.
